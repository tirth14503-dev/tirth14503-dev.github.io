What’s new

Improvements to MarginGuard, newest first.

  1. 22 Nov · Uploads

    Bigger files, and errors that name the file

    Spreadsheets and CSV exports can now be up to 25 MB and 50,000 rows each, with up to 12 files in one upload. If a file can’t be used, the message names the file and says why, and the audit starts with the rest.

  2. 22 Nov · Look and feel

    A simpler menu, plainer words

    The menu now follows the five steps: bring data in, check, decide, get money back and save going forward. Planning, extra checks and analysis are one click away under More tools, or turn on Show all tools. Explanations use plain words, each page has one “How these numbers are worked out” line, and pages move with short, quiet animations that follow your device’s reduce-motion setting.

  3. 22 Nov · Claims

    Track every claim until the money is back

    Start a claim from any overcharge, check the letter and send it from your own mailbox or from MarginGuard’s email. Supplier replies and credit notes are attached to the claim, and Money recovered shows what you claimed, what was promised and what came back. Nothing counts as recovered until you confirm it.

  4. 22 Nov · Payments

    Stop overcharges before you pay them

    Before a payment run, every unpaid invoice is marked Clear, Hold or Blocked, with the reason and the money at stake. Each line is matched to its order and to what arrived, so short or missing deliveries are held. The payment file for your bank lists only cleared and approved invoices, and MarginGuard never pays anyone.

  5. 22 Nov · Savings

    See price rises while they’re small

    Price trends follows each supplier’s prices over time and flags slow creep, sudden jumps of 10% or more, prices above your contract and contracts that end soon, ranked by what they could cost you over the next 12 months. A draft email to start the conversation is ready to copy; MarginGuard doesn’t send it.

  6. 22 Nov · Savings

    Compare with businesses like you, if you choose to share

    Turn on sharing to see how your prices for 16 common items, such as flour, diesel and gloves, compare with at least 10 similar businesses, and which suppliers at least 3 businesses like you buy from. No names, invoice numbers or item codes are shared. MarginGuard also shows suppliers that already charged you less on your own invoices, and UK public-contract suppliers, labelled as not checked.

  7. 22 Nov · Accountants

    One sign-in for all your clients

    Accountants and bookkeepers set up a firm and get a code. Each client enters it to grant read-only access or “may act” access, and can revoke it at any time. The firm dashboard shows every client’s open findings, money at risk and money recovered, and flags who needs attention.

  8. 22 Nov · Security

    Authenticator-app sign-in with recovery codes

    Sign in with a 6-digit code from an authenticator app instead of an emailed one, and keep 10 single-use recovery codes in case you lose your phone. Workspace admins can require an authenticator app for everyone.

  9. 22 Nov · Documents

    Decimal commas read correctly

    Prices written with a decimal comma, such as 0,125 or 12,50, are read as decimals in spreadsheets, documents and price lists, so they no longer show up as false overcharges. A number that could be read two ways is left as written and marked for a look.

  10. 22 Nov · Contracts

    Contract and price-list prices

    Upload a price list or a contract, confirm each price, and MarginGuard checks your invoices against it, including invoices that have no purchase order.

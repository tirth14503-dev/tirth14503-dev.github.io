Recovery calculator

What overbilling costs, and what you keep.

Put in what you spend with suppliers in a year. See an estimate of what may be billed in error, what a recovery-audit firm’s usual share would take of it, and what you would keep.

The error rate is an assumption, not a measurement. We assume 0.1% to 0.5% of supplier spend is billed in error; your own rate may be lower or higher. The calculator also assumes everything found is recovered, and in practice some differences turn out to have a reason. The free scan shows what is in your own invoices.

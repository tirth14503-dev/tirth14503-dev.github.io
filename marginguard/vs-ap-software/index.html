Compare · AP automation software

Keep your system. Check what it lets through.

AP automation suites replace how you approve and pay invoices, and they’re good at stopping a duplicate before it’s paid. MarginGuard replaces nothing: it reads what your system already exports and checks the price, the quantity and the freight on every line, then helps you get the money back.

Straight answer

When an AP suite is the better choice.

If your approvals still run on email and paper, an AP automation suite fixes that, and MarginGuard won’t: it doesn’t route approvals or make payments.

If the approvals already work and the question is whether you were billed the right amount, MarginGuard answers it without changing how your team works.

They can work togetherThe suite runs approvals and payments. MarginGuard checks the lines before the payment run, using the suite’s or your ERP’s exports, and handles the claims.

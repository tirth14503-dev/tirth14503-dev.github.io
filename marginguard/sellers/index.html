How it works

From the spreadsheets you have to an invoice nobody disputes.

  1. 1

    Add your records

    Upload the spreadsheets you already have, as CSV or Excel: customers, items, agreed prices, orders, shipments, invoices and payments. Anything that can’t be read safely is skipped with the reason, never guessed.

  2. 2

    Check before you send

    Every invoice is compared with what was agreed, ordered and shipped, the way your customer will check it. Each finding says what is wrong in one plain sentence and shows the rows it rests on.

  3. 3

    Send it right

    Download the invoice as a PDF and as a standard electronic invoice, and send it yourself. Once it is marked sent it can’t be edited: what went out is kept exactly as it was.

  4. 4

    Answer claims with proof

    Paste or upload a customer’s claim. You see their letter beside what your own records show, whether you agree, and one click prepares the credit note and the reply.

What it does

Everything between the order and the payment.

Today

What needs you today, in one place: invoices to check before sending, who to chase, and the money you’re missing.

Claims desk

Every customer claim in one list, with its deadline. Each is checked against your own records: agree, agree in part, don’t agree, or can’t tell.

Get paid

Who owes what and how late, payments that came in short or twice, reminder text ready to copy, and who promised to pay by when.

Money you’re missing

Deliveries never invoiced, invoices priced below the agreed price, short payments nobody chased, and credits given without a reason. Each with an amount.

Orders and quotes

What is still to deliver and what was promised. A quote below your minimum margin needs an owner or admin to sign it off.

Profit

By customer and by item, from invoices actually sent, after credit notes. Items with no cost on file are counted and shown, never guessed.

How customers see you

Your own scorecard, worked out the way a buyer would: deliveries on time, invoices right first time, and claims and how they ended.

Download everything

Every list and every sent document in one ZIP file, any time. Six roles, and a record of who did what that can’t be quietly altered.

Straight answers

Price

Free during early access. Pricing will be announced before anything is charged.

What it doesn’t do

It sends nothing to your customers and sends no email: you download the files and send them. It doesn’t connect to a bank or an accounting system; your data comes in as CSV or Excel files.

How it decides

Every finding comes from fixed checks on your own records, with the rows it rests on. Anything it can’t read safely is skipped with the reason.

Security

One company never sees another’s records. Two-step sign-in, a recovery code, and sessions that end after 30 minutes unused. Its limits today: no outside penetration test yet, and the database file is not encrypted on disk.

Straight answer

When a recovery-audit firm is the better choice.

If you want someone else to do all of the work, or you have years of past invoices that nobody has the time to go back through, a contingency audit makes sense: you only pay out of what it finds.

MarginGuard needs someone on your side to add the files, or connect a mailbox, and to decide what to claim. It’s for catching overcharges as they happen, not for digging through the archive on your behalf.

They can work togetherMarginGuard on the invoices arriving now, and a look-back audit on the old ones. What MarginGuard has already caught and recovered is no longer there for an audit to find.

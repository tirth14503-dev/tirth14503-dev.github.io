Churn Prediction Model

Machine Learning · Tirthal Kothari

An interpretable decision tree that connects churn signals with proposed retention actions.

The challenge

This machine-learning project used a subscription dataset with tenure, charges, support calls, and contract type to model customer churn and understand its contributing factors.

What I did

I built a decision-tree classifier, tuned depth and minimum leaf size to limit overfitting, and interpreted the tree’s splits to develop proposed retention actions.

The deliverable

An interpretable churn classifier with proposed retention actions based on its decision rules.

Tools & methods

  • scikit-learn
  • Decision Trees

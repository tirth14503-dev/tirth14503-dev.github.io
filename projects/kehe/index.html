Inbound Truckload RFP

Industry Practicum · KeHE · Tirthal Kothari

A lane-level carrier-award recommendation balancing transportation cost, coverage, and service.

The challenge

Our university transportation practicum worked with KeHE Distributors’ real 2024 inbound truckload RFP, including lane-level carrier bids.

What I did

I analyzed lane-level carrier bids across cost, coverage, and service, then developed an award recommendation that balanced rates with reliability and network fit.

The deliverable

A lane-level carrier-award recommendation with a clear rationale for the cost, coverage, and service tradeoffs.

Optimization in Excel

Decision Modeling · Tirthal Kothari

Production planning under capacity and demand constraints, with sensitivity analysis to support decisions.

The challenge

The M&D Chemicals coursework case called for minimizing production cost while meeting demand and capacity constraints.

What I did

I formulated the LP model in Excel Solver, defining decision variables, constraints, and the objective. I then interpreted the sensitivity report and ran what-if scenarios on cost and capacity.

The deliverable

An optimized production plan for the coursework case, supported by sensitivity analysis and cost/capacity scenarios.

Tools & methods

  • Excel Solver
  • Linear Programming
  • Sensitivity Analysis
